How it works

From your photo to your score in a couple of seconds, right on your own device.

📸

You pick a photo

After agreeing to the terms. The AI (about 23 MB) downloads on your first visit, and your browser keeps it for next time.

🧹

We tidy it up

Turn it the right way up, cut off black bars, shrink it so the AI runs fast.

🦶

Is it feet? AI

MobileCLIP, a small AI from Apple that understands images and words, compares your photo with 17 descriptions like "bare feet", "a car", "a face".

✓ Feet (50%+ match) → keep going ↓ ✗ Not feet → stop, no score

Five judges score it, each out of 10

Two use the AI, three are simple measurements of the pixels.
